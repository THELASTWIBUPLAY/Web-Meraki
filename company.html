ABOUT MERAKI STUDIO

An independent creative production studio crafting animation, art, and games from Malang, Indonesia.

“Meraki” describes putting your soul, creativity, and love into what you do. That is how we approach every frame, every asset, and every interaction we make for our partners.

Our story

Meraki Studio began as a small group of artists and developers who wanted to make expressive work with a strong sense of craft. Today we support partners from first concept to final delivery, covering animation, asset production, VFX, and game development, with clear communication at every stage.
